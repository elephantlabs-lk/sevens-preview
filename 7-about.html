About Us

Who We Are

A Horana company, built for Horana families

Sevens Super City (Pvt) Ltd runs a full supermarket on Anguruwathota Road, Wewala, together with a head office and a wholesale section. We are open every day from 7 am to 10 pm.

We began with a simple idea: people in Horana should not have to travel to the city for a clean, well-stocked and fairly priced place to shop. That idea still guides every decision we make.

Our Promise

Serving with Purpose

Sevens Super City exists to make daily life easier for the people of Horana, because every family deserves good food at a fair price.

We look after three things: the freshness of what we sell, the people who work with us, and the town we are part of.
