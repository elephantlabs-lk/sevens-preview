About Us

Who we are
Our company

A Horana business, serving Horana families

Sevens Super City (Pvt) Ltd runs a full supermarket on Anguruwathota Road, Wewala, together with a head office and a wholesale section. We are open every day from 7 am to 10 pm.

We started with a simple idea: people in Horana should not have to travel to the city for a clean, well-stocked, fairly priced place to shop. That idea still guides every decision we make.

Our vision

To be the supermarket Horana is proud to call its own.

A local company trusted by every household and business in the area.

Our mission

Good food, fair prices, helpful people.

To bring fresh food and everyday needs to our community at honest prices, while creating good jobs for local young people.
