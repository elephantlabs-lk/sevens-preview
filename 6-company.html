About us
Who we are

Sevens Super City: Horana's own supermarket

Sevens Super City (Pvt) Ltd is a locally owned supermarket on Anguruwathota Road, Wewala. We bring a full range of fresh food, groceries and household goods to the families of Horana, with a wholesale section that supplies local businesses.

Our aim is simple: a clean, well-stocked store, honest prices, and people who are glad to help.
